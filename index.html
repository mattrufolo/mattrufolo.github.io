About

I'm a PhD researcher at IDSIA (USI-SUPSI, Lugano), working on meta-learning and in-context learning for dynamical systems. My research develops methods for fast adaptation and probabilistic state estimation from noisy, sequential observations — spanning Transformer and state-space architectures, latent dynamics representations, and diffusion-based generative sequence models. I'm particularly interested in the interplay between amortized inference, distributional robustness, and interpretability, and in testing these ideas on real physical systems, from robotic arms to batteries.

Before my PhD, I worked at CERN on large-scale nonlinear physics simulation for LHC luminosity optimization, and completed my MSc in Mathematical Engineering at Politecnico di Torino (110/110 cum laude), with an exchange internship at the Basque Center of Applied Mathematics (BCAM).
